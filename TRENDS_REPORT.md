# Trend report: lifestyle · fashion · fitness · travel
*TikTok & Instagram Reels, status October 2026*

> Note: most of the sources are marketing and creator blogs. The direction is consistent across them, but treat individual numbers as indicative, not as official platform data.

## 1. What the algorithms reward right now (and therefore why things work)

| | Instagram Reels | TikTok |
|---|---|---|
| **Top signals** | Watch time, **sends per reach** (DM shares), likes per reach | Completion rate, rewatches, shares, comments |
| **Hashtags** | Max ~5, mainly for categorizing; caption keywords count more | 3–5 specific tags; **no #fyp/#viral** |
| **Search/SEO** | Caption keywords | Caption, **on-screen text and speech** are all indexed |
| **Shift** | Away from likes, towards "is this worth sending to someone" | Towards search: videos are found for months through keywords |

**What this means for you:**
- **Seamless loops**: rewatches push watch time up. The editor ends every video on the moment just before the first shot.
- **First 2 seconds = show the outcome**: no slow intro. The editor puts the strongest, most energetic shot first, with hook text.
- **Make it sendable**: captions with "send this to…" encourage DM shares, the strongest Instagram signal.
- **Keywords in the first caption line and on screen**: "Leg day routine" beats "vibes ✨".

## 2. Fitness
**Hot right now:** functional training, strength training for everyone (not just "bulking"), Pilates, 30-day challenges.
**Works:** short, practical clips people **save** to try themselves (form tips, "3 exercises for…"), transformations/progress, challenges with a day counter.
**Why:** saves and rewatches are high when a video is useful. Progress stories create follow-intent ("I want to see how this ends").
**Your formats:** `day X/30`, `wrong vs right`, `gym POV`, `progress check-in`.

## 3. Travel
**Hot right now:** cost breakdowns ("what I spent in 1 day"), lesser-known destinations (saturated: Western Europe/Mexico), POV itineraries, photo slideshows on trending audio.
**Not anymore:** a pretty drone shot as the hook. Everyone has those now.
**Why:** a number or a specific situation creates curiosity ("how is that so cheap?"). Honest, slightly raw footage feels more trustworthy than over-polished.
**Your formats:** `1 day in…`, `what I spent`, `hidden spot reveal`, `save this for your trip`.

## 4. Fashion
**Hot right now:** physical transitions (ceiling-fan swap, shoe toss, OOTD "stop" walk-around), **outfits of the week** with the same action in every cut, occasion-based (first day of October, college, fall).
**Aesthetics:** gorpcore, 90s grunge, sneaker revival, dark academia, statement jewelry.
**Why:** a repeated action makes fast cuts satisfying (pattern + variation). An occasion gives people a reason to care about the clothes.
**Your formats:** `outfits of the week`, `3 ways to style 1 piece`, `rate this fit`.

## 5. Lifestyle
**Hot right now:** day in my life, weekend reset, routines, "small habits".
**Works:** when it's **specific and real** (no generic B-roll), with relatable text that people send to friends.
**Why:** the platforms reward total watch time and shares over likes. Relatable = sendable.

## 6. How this is built into AutoEdit
- `trends.yaml` contains the hooks, formats, keywords and hashtags per niche, and the editor and caption generator use them.
- Hook text comes from these banks, and A/B/C variants each test a different hook.
- Captions: keywords first, max 5 hashtags, no generic tags, and a call to send or save.
- Performance feedback (`autoedit stats`) weighs **shares and saves heavier than likes** and adjusts hashtags and posting times automatically.
